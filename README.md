# Customer Churn Analysis – Telecom Industry

## Business Problem

The company has been seeing a steady rise in customer churn, which is affecting recurring revenue and long-term customer value. Even though new customers continue to join, many of them leave early, especially within the first few months.
The goal of this analysis was to understand why customers are churning and identify practical ways to improve retention.

## Dataset

IBM Telco Customer Churn Dataset

## Key Insights

- Early tenure customers churn the most
- Month-to-month contracts drive churn
- Payment friction increases churn
- Value-added services reduce churn

## Business Recommendations

- Focus retention efforts on **new customers**, especially in the first 1–3 months.
- Encourage customers to move from **month-to-month plans to longer-term contracts** using discounts or bundled offers.
- Reduce payment-related issues by promoting **auto-pay options**.
- Bundle **support and protection services** into early plans to increase perceived value and reliability.

## Tools Used

Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn
